LIVE ALIEN — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-13

About this page

This page explains how LIVE ALIEN, a game by Tagongo Games, handles information, and how you can delete your account and your data. It adds to the general Tagongo Games Privacy Policy (https://tagongo.com/privacy), which also applies. Tagongo Games is the controller of the information described here. Questions and requests: tagongo@gmail.com.

In short

LIVE ALIEN never asks for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. You can play the whole game without any online feature. If you have a Google Play Games profile, the game signs in with it when it opens and turns on the cloud save, so your game is stored online and you can get it back on another phone; you can cancel that sign-in, or delete your cloud data in Settings at any time. If you turn on Friends, people you accept can see your creature's card, visit it and send it a gift. There is no chat and no free text between players. The game offers optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. There is one optional purchase, Premium, handled by Google Play: we never receive your payment details. We do not sell personal information. You can delete your account and your online data at any time, inside the game or by writing to us (see "How to delete your account and your data").

Who the game is for

LIVE ALIEN is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children and is not part of Google Play's Families program. The game does not ask for your age or date of birth. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in their country: the online features (cloud save and Friends) are meant for players of that age and over. Friends stays off until you turn it on; the cloud save turns on by itself only when the phone already has a Google Play Games profile (see "Cloud save"). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the online features, write to tagongo@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and we will delete the account and its data (see "How to delete your account and your data").

Cloud save (optional — Google Firebase)

When the game opens, it tries to sign you in with Google Play Games. If you have a Play Games profile and do not cancel, the cloud save turns on by itself. You can also turn it on later in Settings, with Google Play Games or with a rescue code (it is not an e-mail address and you never choose a password). If you delete your cloud data in Settings, the cloud save stays off until you turn it on again. Google Firebase Authentication then gives the game a user ID, which is your account for the online features, and Google Cloud Firestore stores under that ID: your saved game and its backup copies; your creature's details (the name you gave it, its looks, age and stage, and your time-zone offset so the game knows when it is night for it); the model of your phone, so you can tell your devices apart; and your private diary. The diary is text you write yourself and only you can read it: please do not write personal details in it. If the cloud save is never turned on, none of this leaves your phone.

Friends (optional)

Friends is off until you turn it on; it needs the cloud save account and opens only after your creature has hatched. When you turn it on, the game creates a friend code for you (8 random characters) and stores online, under your user ID: your creature's card (the name you gave it, its looks and shape, stage, age in days, mood, and whether it is asleep, overweight or dirty); your friend list and your block list (kept as friend codes and user IDs, never names); the invitations, visit requests and gifts waiting for you; and short daily marks that enforce limits such as one gift per friend per day. Friendship works by invitation and both sides must accept. A person you invite sees your friend code, your creature's name and its looks. Only the friends you accepted can see your creature's card. Your friends and the people you invite also receive your user ID, a random-looking code that does not say who you are. Nobody else can see anything, and codes cannot be searched or listed. A visit leaves a knock that you accept or decline, gifts are chosen from a fixed list (food, a toy or a surprise), and you can remove or block anyone, unblock them, stop receiving invitations, get a new code or turn Friends off, which closes your card. There is no chat, no free text, no voice and no pictures between players. The only text another player can see is your creature's name, which is filtered and limited to 10 letters: please do not use your real name.

Google Play Games

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google gives the game your Play Games player ID. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account. We never receive your Google password or your e-mail address.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The only ads in the game are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. To have a video ready, the game asks Google AdMob for one when it starts, so AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, IP address (which shows your approximate location), device information, diagnostics and your interactions with ads, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, even if you never watch a video. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Ads are not personalized unless you have given consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in other places where the law requires it, the game asks you first through Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (Google Play)

The game offers one optional one-time purchase, Premium, which removes the ads and unlocks all the room items. It can also be redeemed with a Google Play promo code. The purchase is processed by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy): we never receive or store your card number or any other payment details. Google Play tells the game only that the purchase was made, so that the game can unlock Premium (performance of a contract, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). The "Premium" status is stored on your phone and, if the cloud save is on, in your cloud save, where it is deleted together with it.

Game statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count simple game events (for example: a creature hatched, a creature was named, a gift video was offered, declined or watched) with an app-instance ID. It does not use your advertising ID, your creature's name or anything you write. Where the law requires consent, statistics stay off unless you agree in the consent form described under "Rewarded ads", and you can withdraw that consent at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. The game also uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports when the game fails: technical details of the error, game version, device model and Android version, with a random installation ID. Crash reports do not depend on that consent.

Reminders

The first time you open the game, Android asks whether to allow notifications (on Android 13 and later). If you allow them, reminders (for example when your egg hatches) are created and scheduled on your phone only. They do not use our servers, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your phone's settings.

Game clock

The game checks the current time with a Google server (the same servers used by the cloud save) so that changing the phone's clock does not change how fast your creature grows. This request sends no data beyond what any web request carries (your IP address) and stores nothing about you. Without an internet connection, the game relies on the phone's own uptime instead.

What stays on your phone

Your game, settings, reminder choices and, if you do not use the cloud save, your diary are stored on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them from the phone, but not the online data: delete that first (see "How to delete your account and your data").

Why we use information and legal bases

To provide the features you ask for — cloud save and Friends (performance of a contract, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)); to keep the service secure, prevent abuse of Friends and fix crashes (our legal obligations and our legitimate interest, art. 6(1)(c) and (f)); to measure how the game is used and to show the rewarded ads you choose to watch (your consent where the law requires it, art. 6(1)(a); otherwise our legitimate interest in improving and funding a free game).

Service providers and international transfers

Google provides Firebase as our data processor; Google Play, Google Play Games and Google AdMob are provided under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The cloud save and Friends database is hosted in the European Union. Google may also process information in other countries, such as the United States, relying on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. We do not share information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long information is kept

Your account, cloud save, backup copies, creature card, friend and block lists and diary stay online until you delete them; they do not expire by themselves. Invitations, visit requests and gifts are removed when you answer or open them. The daily limit marks are replaced every day. Information on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the game. If you write to us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it and to show that we handled your request. Google keeps its own records, including advertising, analytics and crash data, according to its policies.

How to delete your account and your data

Inside the game: open Settings → Cloud save → Delete my cloud data and confirm twice. This deletes your account (the Firebase user) and everything stored with it: your saved game, its backup copies, your diary, your creature's details and, if you used Friends, your creature's card, friend code, friend list, block list and pending invitations, visits and gifts. Your friends stop seeing your creature at once. The game on your phone is not touched, and you can keep playing without the online features. Without the game: write to tagongo@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and include your rescue code or your friend code so we can find your account (we do not have your name or e-mail address in the game). If you have neither code, tell us and we will help you; you can also install the game again, sign in and use the option above. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm it to you. Deleting only part of your data, without deleting the account: you can delete single diary pages from the diary itself, remove a friend, get a new friend code or turn Friends off, which closes your card. What may remain: the daily limit marks of the day of deletion (shortened codes that contain no information about anyone) can stay for a short time until they are cleared; Google keeps its own advertising, analytics and crash records according to its policies; saved data kept by Google Play Games is managed from the Play Games app; and advertising data is controlled with your advertising ID in the Android settings. If the cloud save was never turned on, nothing is stored online: uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes everything.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or port your information, to restrict or object to its processing, and you can withdraw consent at any time; you can also complain to your data-protection authority. We answer within 30 days, free of charge. This includes the rights of the GDPR and UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA (we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising; ads are not personalized unless you consent), Brazil's LGPD and similar laws.

Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The database rules let each player read and write only their own data, a friend you accepted read only the card you share, and other players only leave an invitation, a visit request or a gift within fixed limits.

Changes and contact

We may update this page when the game or the law changes; the current version and its date are always here. Contact: tagongo@gmail.com.
