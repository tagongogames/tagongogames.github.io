Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-30

Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Tagongo Games ("Tagongo Games", "we", "us") handles information in every game and app we publish on Google Play and on this website. Tagongo Games decides why and how this information is processed (it is the "controller" under the GDPR and similar data-protection laws). Some features described here are used only by some games; each section says when that is the case. For any privacy question or request, write to tagongo@gmail.com.

Our approach in short

Our games are designed to collect as little as possible. You never create a Tagongo Games account. We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, address, contacts, photos or files, we never see your passwords or payment-card details, and we do not sell personal information. Your game progress and settings are stored on your own device. Some information is processed automatically by the Google services built into our games (advertising, purchases and Google Play), as explained below.

Information processed automatically

Depending on the game, your device and your choices, Google services integrated in our games may process: device and app information (model, operating-system version, app version, language, country); approximate location derived from your IP address; advertising and device identifiers; in-app interactions (for example which screens are opened and whether an ad was shown or tapped); and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance. This information is used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and security, and it is encrypted in transit. Tagongo Games does not receive most of it directly. On recent Android versions the advertising SDK may also use the Android Privacy Sandbox APIs, which you can control in your device's privacy settings. Our games do not request precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or file-storage permissions.

Data stored on your device

Games keep their progress on your device: levels and stars, scores, unlocked items and purchases, in-game coins, settings (sound, music, language, accessibility), tutorial progress and similar game data. This data stays on your device. It is deleted if you uninstall the game, clear its storage or use an in-game reset, and we cannot recover it afterwards.

Why we process information and on what legal basis

Running the game, saving your progress and restoring purchases: to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract) and our legitimate interest in games that work well. Personalized ads: only with your consent where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). Non-personalized or contextual ads, ad measurement, fraud prevention and security: our legitimate interests in funding free games and keeping them safe. Purchases: performance of a contract and legal obligations (for example tax records kept by Google Play). Answering your e-mails: our legitimate interest in replying to you, or steps you ask us to take. Complying with the law and protecting our rights: legal obligations and legitimate interests. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests at any time by writing to us.

Advertising and your consent (Google AdMob)

Games that show ads use Google AdMob (banners, full-screen ads at natural breaks and optional rewarded videos). Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform, a consent tool that follows the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from "Privacy options" in the game's settings when it is shown, and on any Android device you can delete or reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in the device settings. Declining personalized ads does not remove ads; you may still see non-personalized or contextual ads. Rewarded videos are always optional. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

Unless a game's own privacy page says otherwise, our games are intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and are not part of Google Play's "Designed for Families" program; games for a general audience that includes children use a neutral age screen and a limited mode, described on their own page. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (the age set by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) or below the age of digital consent in their country (between 13 and 16 in the European Union). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, write to tagongo@gmail.com and we will delete it. If we ever publish a game directed to children, it will follow COPPA (including its 2025 amendments), the GDPR rules for children and Google Play's Families Policy: no personalized ads, only ad services certified for families, no collection of children's personal information without verifiable parental consent, and purchases and external links behind a parental gate. This policy will then name those games. Parents can also use Google Play's parental controls and purchase authentication.

TIDYBOLT: online rankings

TIDYBOLT has free online rankings and a weekly community goal with their own privacy page, which adds to this policy: Google Firebase with an anonymous player ID and your scores, no account, no chat and nothing that identifies you: https://tagongo.com/tidybolt/privacy.

Games with online features

Some games have online features with their own privacy page, which adds to this policy: VEILHORDE (VEIL DUEL online duels with Google Firebase, an anonymous player ID and no chat): https://tagongo.com/veilhorde/privacy.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (for example removing ads, content packs or in-game coins) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive your card number or bank details; Google gives the game only the minimum needed to confirm, unlock and restore your purchase. Eligible non-consumable purchases can be restored from the same Google account.

Google Play and Google Play Games

Our games are distributed and updated through Google Play, which may process information for installation, licensing, security and integrity. Some games offer Google Play Games features such as sign-in, achievements or leaderboards; when you use them, Google processes the data of your Play Games profile under its own terms, and we never receive your Google password. Some games include Firebase components that are bundled with Google's advertising and Play libraries; we do not use Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics to track you. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is processed by the service providers that make our games work — mainly Google (AdMob, Google Play, Google Play Games and related Firebase components) — under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights, safety or property of players or of Tagongo Games, or as part of a merger or transfer of our games, in which case this policy will continue to protect it.

Our website: cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are useful, but only if you accept it in the cookie notice. Until you choose, and if you reject, the analytics script is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_*) and receives the pages you visit, clicks on links, scrolling, the page you came from, your browser, device type and language, and your approximate country or region. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We have turned off Google signals, ads personalization and granular location and device data; the data is not used for advertising, and user-level data is deleted after 2 months (aggregated reports without identifiers may be kept longer). Google acts as our processor under the Google Ads Data Processing Terms and may process the data in other countries, such as the United States, with the safeguards described under "Security and international transfers". The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page; this also deletes the analytics cookies. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a rejection and do not ask; we do not otherwise respond to "Do Not Track" signals, because analytics only runs with your consent. The site also stores your language, accessibility and cookie choices in your browser (local storage), which you can clear at any time. Our hosting provider (Netlify) may keep standard technical server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and operation: https://www.netlify.com/privacy/ Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How long information is kept

We do not keep a central database of players. Data on your device remains until you delete it. E-mails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and afterwards for up to two years, unless the law requires otherwise. Information processed by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Security and international transfers

We design our games to avoid unnecessary data collection, and data sent by the games is encrypted in transit. We protect the little information we hold (such as e-mails) with access control and encrypted storage, in line with the GDPR and the other data-security laws that apply to us, and we will notify authorities and affected people of a serious security incident when the law requires it. Google may process information in other countries; for transfers from the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom it relies on safeguards recognized by law, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, and you can withdraw consent at any time (for ads, through "Privacy options" or your device settings). Depending on your country you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to a data-protection authority. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing. To exercise a right, write to tagongo@gmail.com; it is free, we may ask for information needed to confirm the request, and we will answer within 30 days (or sooner if your local law requires it). Because we hold little or no data that identifies you, many requests are handled through Google's settings (https://myadcenter.google.com) or by resetting your device's advertising ID; we will help you with that.

Other countries

Wherever you live, we process information in accordance with the data-protection laws that apply to us and to you. You are not under any legal obligation to give us information, and our games do not ask you for any. The purposes for which information is used and the parties that receive it are described in this policy. You have the right to review information about you that we hold and to ask for its correction or deletion, and you can contact the data-protection authority of your country.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection apply to you, with the rights and legal bases described above. You can complain to the data-protection authority of the country where you live or work: EU authorities are listed at https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en and in the United Kingdom it is the Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk).

United States (California and other states)

This section is for residents of California (CCPA/CPRA and CalOPPA) and of other U.S. states with privacy laws. In the last 12 months the Google services in our games may have processed these categories of personal information: identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (in-app interactions with ads), approximate location derived from the IP address, and inferences used to select ads. They come from your device and are used for the purposes described above. We do not sell personal information for money and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information. Showing personalized ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws: you can opt out at any time by declining personalized ads in the consent message or "Privacy options", or by deleting or resetting your advertising ID or turning off ads personalization in your Android settings. We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16. You have the rights to know, access, correct and delete, and to opt out, and we will not discriminate against you for using them. You can send a request (or an authorized agent can send one) to tagongo@gmail.com, and if we refuse it you may appeal by replying to our answer.

Brazil (LGPD)

If you are in Brazil, the General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) applies. We process data on the legal bases described above: performance of a contract, legitimate interest, consent (for example for personalized ads and website analytics) and compliance with legal obligations. You can ask us to confirm whether we process your data, and to access, correct, anonymize, block or delete it, to port it, to know with whom it is shared, and to revoke your consent, by writing to tagongo@gmail.com, which is our channel of communication with data subjects. You can also petition the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD, https://www.gov.br/anpd). Data is transferred abroad, mainly to Google in the United States, for the purposes described in this policy and with the safeguards described under "Security and international transfers".

Mexico

For users in Mexico, this policy is our privacy notice under the Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties. It describes the data we process, its purposes and the service providers that receive it. We do not process sensitive personal data. You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, cancellation and opposition (ARCO rights), revoke your consent or limit the use or disclosure of your data by writing to tagongo@gmail.com; we will answer within the time limits set by law. Changes to this notice are published on this page.

Republic of Korea

For users in Korea, under the Personal Information Protection Act: (1) purposes and items of personal information are those described in this policy; (2) retention and destruction: we keep no central database of players; e-mails are kept for up to two years and then permanently deleted, and electronic records are erased so that they cannot be recovered; (3) entrustment and transfer abroad: to provide the games we entrust processing to Google LLC (United States): AdMob for advertising (advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, in-app interactions and diagnostics), Google Play Billing for payments (purchase records) and, in games with online features, Firebase (anonymous player ID and match data); the information is sent over the network when you use these features and is kept according to Google's policies; you can limit it by declining personalized ads in "Privacy options", resetting your advertising ID or not using online features, but some functions may then be unavailable; (4) you can ask at any time for access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing; (5) person in charge of privacy: Tagongo Games privacy contact, tagongo@gmail.com. You can also contact the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (https://www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972) or the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (https://privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118).

Japan

For users in Japan, we handle personal information in accordance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information. We entrust the handling of the personal data needed to provide the games (such as advertising identifiers, purchase records and online-play data) to Google LLC, which may process it in the United States and other countries under its own privacy and security program. Information for personalized ads is provided to advertising partners only with your consent where required, and you can change your choice in "Privacy options". You can ask for disclosure, correction, suspension of use or deletion of your personal data by writing to tagongo@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our games or the law change. The current version, with its effective date, is always published at https://tagongo.com/privacy. Important changes will be published here before they apply and, where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent again. Older versions of some games link to our previous privacy page (https://sites.google.com/view/tagongoprivacypolicy/); this policy is the current one and replaces it.
